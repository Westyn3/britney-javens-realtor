Our Story

Meet the
Legacy Team

A mother-daughter real estate duo with generational insight, local expertise, and a commitment to treating every client like family.

The Agent

Britney Javens

Britney brings a modern, energetic, and solutions-focused approach to real estate. With a strong background in client care, local market insight, and digital marketing, she plays a vital role in assisting buyers and sellers navigate the fast-moving landscape of North Atlanta.

Clients love working with Britney for her kindness, responsiveness, and unwavering commitment to making every experience smooth and stress-free. She is deeply invested in helping families along the GA-400 corridor — whether it's a luxury estate in Milton, a walkable Buckhead or Brookhaven address, a family neighborhood in Roswell or Johns Creek, or new construction in fast-growing Cumming.

  • 4+ years of real estate experience
  • Background in flipping and renovating homes
  • Deep knowledge of North Atlanta market trends
  • Expert in buyer & seller negotiations

The Partner

Karen Hilliard

Meet Karen Hilliard — Britney's teammate, mother, and trusted partner. Karen leads the team's Lake Lanier and North Georgia markets, from Gainesville, Buford, and Flowery Branch to Dahlonega and Dawsonville, with deep expertise in lakefront, luxury, and mountain properties.

Karen's experience spans decades of North Georgia real estate. She brings wisdom, warmth, and an unmatched network of local connections — so when your search heads north toward the lake or the mountains, the Legacy Team already has you covered.

  • Decades of North Georgia real estate experience
  • Lake Lanier waterfront specialist — docks, coves & lake living
  • Deep local connections across Hall, Gwinnett & Dawson counties
  • Trusted resource for relocation & lifestyle guidance
