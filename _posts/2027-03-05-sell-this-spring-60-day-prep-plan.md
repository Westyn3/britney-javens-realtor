Spring is the busiest selling season in North Atlanta, and the sellers who benefit most aren't the ones who list first — they're the ones who list *ready*. If you're thinking about listing this spring, here's a 60-day plan. (If you're still deciding whether to sell now or wait, start with [Should You Sell Now or Wait Until Spring?](/blog/sell-now-or-wait-until-spring/).)

## Days 60–45: Get the facts

- **Get a market evaluation.** You need a realistic price range based on recent comparable sales in your specific neighborhood.
- **Walk the house like a buyer.** Make a list of everything you'd notice: worn paint, stained grout, loose fixtures, dated lighting.
- **Decide what's worth fixing.** Not every project pays for itself. I come from a family of home builders and trained on renovations, so I'll tell you honestly which updates matter and which are wasted money.

## Days 45–30: Fix and declutter

- **Handle the repairs first.** Leaky faucets, sticking doors, and cracked caulk signal neglect to buyers and inspectors.
- **Declutter aggressively.** Pack away personal items, thin out furniture, and clear counters.
- **Deep clean.** Windows, baseboards, and carpets make a bigger impact than most people expect.
- **Paint selectively.** Fresh neutral paint in high-impact rooms is often one of the best-value improvements.

## Days 30–15: Curb appeal and systems

- **Clean up the exterior.** Pressure wash, refresh mulch, trim shrubs, and fix the front door.
- **Service the basics.** HVAC servicing and clear gutters help avoid surprises later.
- **Consider a pre-listing inspection.** Knowing about issues early lets you fix or price accordingly, rather than being surprised during due diligence.

## Days 15–7: Prepare the paperwork

- **Gather documents.** Warranties, permits, receipts for major work, and HOA information.
- **Complete your disclosures.** Georgia sellers have disclosure responsibilities for known defects, so be thorough and honest.
- **Plan your move.** Decide where you'll live if the house sells quickly.

## Days 7–0: Photos and launch

- **Schedule professional photography** when the home and yard look their best.
- **Finalize your list price** with your agent using the most current data.
- **Plan showings and the first weekend.** In competitive neighborhoods, the first weekend can decide the outcome — as I explain from the buyer side in [how to win a multiple-offer situation](/blog/winning-multiple-offers-north-atlanta/).

## Common mistakes to avoid

1. Pricing based on what you need rather than what buyers will pay
2. Over-renovating just before listing
3. Ignoring small repairs that signal neglect
4. Skipping professional photography
5. Listing before the house is truly ready
