If you've toured Alpharetta, you've heard the word *Windward* — usually in the same breath as "the lake." But Windward is bigger and more varied than any one feature. It's a master-planned community of more than three thousand homes, and treating it as a single neighborhood is the most common mistake buyers make. Here's how to read it.

## The anchors

Three things define Windward:

- **Lake Windward** — a 195-acre lake that gives the community its identity. Homes on or near the water carry their own premium, and "lake view" can mean very different things from one street to the next.
- **The golf club** — the private Golf Club of Georgia sits within the Windward orbit. Membership is separate from owning a home, so budget for it as its own line item if golf matters to you.
- **The sports park and swim/tennis scene** — Windward supports one of the busiest swim/tennis ecosystems in North Fulton, which matters enormously to families with kids.

## The sections are different markets

Windward's depth is the reason it holds its value: there's a buyer for almost every budget, which keeps resale demand steady across seasons. Roughly:

- **Townhomes and established family homes** start in the $500s and $600s.
- **Executive homes** run through the $800s and $900s.
- **Lakefront estates** trade well beyond $2M.

Two homes with the same square footage can sit a long way apart in price depending on section, lot, and water access. This is the single most important thing to understand before you make an offer — the comps have to be picked street by street, not neighborhood-wide.

## The location advantage

Windward has direct access to GA-400 at exit 11, and Avalon and downtown Alpharetta are about ten minutes away. The Windward Parkway retail corridor handles daily errands. For commuters and relocating families, that convenience is a big part of why the tours keep ending here.

## What to check before you buy

1. **Which section, exactly.** HOA dues, amenities, and lot sizes vary — confirm what your specific address includes.
2. **Zoning for schools.** Always verify the school zoning for the specific address with Fulton County Schools. Don't rely on a listing sheet.
3. **Lake access vs. lake view.** Get clarity on what rights come with a home, and what's simply a view.
4. **Membership costs.** If you want the golf club, understand the membership process and fees up front.

## Is Windward right for you?

Windward fits buyers who want amenities, resale liquidity, and a convenient GA-400 location — and who are comfortable with a large, active community instead of a quiet cul-de-sac.
