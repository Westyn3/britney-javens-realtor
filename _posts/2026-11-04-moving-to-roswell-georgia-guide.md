## Why People Love Living in Roswell

### Historic Downtown and Canton Street

The heart of Roswell is its historic downtown, and the heart of downtown is Canton Street. This walkable stretch is lined with locally owned restaurants, sidewalk cafes, boutiques, and galleries, many of them tucked into beautifully restored historic homes. It's been named a Georgia Great Street, and once you spend an evening there, you'll understand why.

Roswell is known for its dining scene, with close to 200 independent restaurants across the city. Canton Street is home to local favorites like Table &amp; Main, which earned a Michelin Bib Gourmand, along with spots for Italian, Southern comfort food, seafood, and cocktails.

From April through October, Canton Street hosts Alive in Roswell on the third Thursday of each month, a street festival with live music, vendors, and family-friendly fun. It's one of my favorite ways to introduce clients to the community.

### History You Can Walk Through

Roswell's history is part of everyday life here. The downtown historic district covers roughly 640 acres, and the city is home to three historic house museums: Bulloch Hall, Barrington Hall, and Smith Plantation. It's the kind of place where you can grab coffee, walk past 19th-century landmarks, and end up at a great dinner, all in the same afternoon.

### Riverside Parks and Trails

For a city so close to Atlanta, Roswell has a remarkable amount of green space, much of it along the Chattahoochee River.

- **Old Mill Park and Vickery Creek:** Explore the ruins of the historic Roswell Mill, cross the covered pedestrian bridge, and hike to the Vickery Creek waterfall, a spillway dam built by mill workers in the 1850s. The trails connect into the Chattahoochee River National Recreation Area.
- **Riverside Park and the Roswell Riverwalk:** Paved paths along the Chattahoochee are perfect for walking, running, biking, and watching the kayakers and paddleboarders float by.
- **Chattahoochee Nature Center:** A local treasure for families, with trails, wildlife exhibits, and river programs.

### Schools and Education

Roswell is served by Fulton County Schools, and many of its public schools are well regarded. The area is also home to a number of respected private schools, including Blessed Trinity Catholic High School, Fellowship Christian School, St. Francis Schools, and High Meadows School, giving families plenty of options.

As always, school assignments depend on the specific address, so I'll help you confirm the zoned schools for any home you're considering.

### Easy Access to Alpharetta, Atlanta, and Beyond

Roswell sits right along GA-400 and close to major routes like Holcomb Bridge Road, which makes it easy to get to [Alpharetta](/alpharetta.html), [Sandy Springs](/sandy-springs.html), Perimeter Center, and Atlanta for work or play. For many commuters, Roswell hits the sweet spot: close enough to the city to be convenient, but with a hometown feel you notice the minute you exit the highway.

## Finding the Right Home in Roswell

Roswell offers an impressive variety of homes:

- **Historic and in-town homes** near Canton Street, where you can walk to dinner and festivals.
- **Established neighborhoods** with mature trees, larger lots, and swim and tennis communities.
- **Townhomes and newer construction** for buyers who want lower maintenance and modern finishes.
- **Riverside and estate properties** for those who want more space and privacy, including the East Roswell and Willeo Road areas.

The right choice comes down to your priorities: walkability, schools, commute, lot size, HOA amenities, and budget. Because Roswell is so popular, well-priced homes in desirable neighborhoods can go quickly, so it pays to have an agent who knows the area street by street.
