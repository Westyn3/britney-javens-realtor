Brookhaven sits in that sweet spot buyers keep hunting for: genuinely intown convenience with neighborhoods that still feel like neighborhoods.

## The lay of the land

Brookhaven isn't one uniform market. The blocks around Town Brookhaven and the Village run walkable and lively. Historic Brookhaven, bordering the Capital City Club golf course, is one of Atlanta's classic prestige addresses. North Brookhaven — the pocket buyers increasingly search for by name — trends newer, with townhome communities and renovated ranches on quieter streets. Each pocket shops differently, and prices swing meaningfully between them.

## Who's moving here

Young families who want intown life without Midtown density, professionals commuting to Buckhead or the Pill Hill medical corridor, and downsizers who want restaurants within walking distance. The common thread: people buying a daily life, not just a house.

## What to watch as a buyer

Brookhaven inventory moves quickly in the popular pockets, and the difference between a great block and an okay one can be a single street. This is a market where touring with someone who knows the pockets — and hears about listings early — genuinely changes your options.
