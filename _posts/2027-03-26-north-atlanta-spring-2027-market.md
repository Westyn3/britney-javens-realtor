Spring is when the North Atlanta market wakes up: more listings, more showings, and more competition. I don't believe in predicting where prices will go, because nobody reliably can. What I can do is tell you what to watch and how to position yourself, depending on which side of the transaction you're on.

(For current local numbers, ask me directly — I track the market weekly, and the data changes faster than any blog post.)

## The four things I watch every week

1. **New listings.** How many homes are coming on the market in your area and price range?
2. **Days on market.** Are homes selling quickly, or sitting?
3. **Price reductions.** More reductions can signal sellers who started too high.
4. **Pending sales.** What's actually going under contract, and at what price relative to list?

Those four tell you more than any headline.

## If you're buying

- **Be ready before you tour.** Fully underwritten pre-approval and clear priorities make you a stronger buyer. My guide on [how to win a multiple-offer situation](/blog/winning-multiple-offers-north-atlanta/) covers tactics.
- **Move fast in popular areas.** Certain neighborhoods sell on a faster clock — I explain which in [the fastest-selling Alpharetta neighborhoods](/blog/fastest-selling-neighborhoods-alpharetta/).
- **Don't ignore the "boring" listings.** Homes that have sat a little longer can offer more negotiating room.
- **Know your walk-away number.** Spring excitement can push buyers past their limits.

## If you're selling

- **Price to the market, not your hopes.** Overpricing in spring costs you the all-important first weeks. See my [60-day prep plan](/blog/sell-this-spring-60-day-prep-plan/).
- **Prepare thoroughly.** Buyers are comparing you to every other listing that weekend.
- **Think about timing.** The first weekend matters in competitive neighborhoods.
- **Have a plan for your next home.** Selling and buying at the same time takes coordination.

## Why local data beats national headlines

National news talks about "the housing market" as if it were one thing. North Atlanta isn't one market — it's dozens of small ones, each behaving differently. A story that's true for the metro can be false for your street. That's why I'd rather look at your neighborhood and price band than quote a headline.

## The bottom line

Spring rewards preparation. Buyers who are underwritten and decisive win more often. Sellers who prepare and price realistically get stronger results. If you want a plan for your specific situation, that's exactly what a conversation is for.
