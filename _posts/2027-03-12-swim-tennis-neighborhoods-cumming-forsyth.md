If you're touring Cumming, you'll hear "swim/tennis community" in nearly every listing. It sounds simple — a pool and some courts — but amenities differ widely, and they're a big part of why neighborhoods feel so different from one another. Here's how to read them.

## What "swim/tennis" usually includes

Most swim/tennis communities offer some combination of a pool, tennis courts, a clubhouse, and a playground, often with a community calendar of events. The differences lie in scale, upkeep, and culture. Some are small and quiet. Some are huge and busy.

## A few Cumming examples

- **[Fieldstone](/fieldstone.html)** is one of Cumming's largest swim/tennis communities, with resort-scale facilities that serve hundreds of families, and homes that typically trade in the $400s to $600s — one of the more attainable entry points into Forsyth County.
- **[Three Chimneys Farm](/three-chimneys-farm.html)** is an established mainstay with sidewalks, mature landscaping, and an active neighborhood calendar. Turnover tends to be modest, with homes typically in the $500s to $700s.
- **[James Creek](/james-creek.html)** offers craftsman-style homes in south Forsyth's school-driven corridor, typically in the $500s to $800s.
- **[Chattahoochee River Club](/chattahoochee-river-club.html)** is an established community on larger wooded lots with river-oriented trails and green space, typically from the $500s to the $900s.
- **[Hampton Golf Village](/hampton-golf-village.html)** blends golf-course scenery with a village feel and a more attainable price point, typically in the $400s to $700s.

(Price ranges are general and change with the market — ask for a current snapshot.)

## Questions to ask about any amenity package

1. **What do the dues cover?** Compare what you're paying for across communities.
2. **How crowded is it?** A massive pool is great until it's packed every Saturday in July.
3. **Who maintains it?** Look at the condition of the facilities and the HOA's financial health.
4. **What are the rules?** Guest policies, rental limits, and architectural standards affect daily life.
5. **Is the culture right?** Visit on a weekend. Notice who's there and how the place feels.

## Why this matters for resale

In Forsyth, strong amenities combined with school access tend to support steady resale demand. But amenities alone aren't a guarantee. Condition, management, and location matter just as much.
