If you ask a longtime Alpharetta family where they live, they usually won't lead with a street name — they'll name their neighborhood. That's because Alpharetta life runs through its swim/tennis communities: the pool in summer, ALTA tennis in spring and fall, and a built-in social circle for kids and parents alike.

## Why swim/tennis matters here

These neighborhoods — hundreds of them across North Fulton — come with amenities and, just as importantly, community. Neighborhood swim teams, tennis nights, food-truck Fridays. For families relocating in, buying into an active swim/tennis neighborhood is the fastest shortcut to feeling at home.

## Shopping by school zone

The other axis local families shop on is the attendance zone. Alpharetta and the surrounding area feed some of Georgia's most sought-after public schools, and two homes a mile apart can zone to different schools entirely. Attendance boundaries can also change over time, so verify the current zoning for any specific address before you fall in love with it — that's part of my job on every buyer search.
