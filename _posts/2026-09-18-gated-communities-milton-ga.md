Milton is North Atlanta's capital of gated living. Large-lot zoning and horse-country privacy created the setting, and a handful of communities define the market — each with its own personality and price of entry.

## The Manor Golf & Country Club

Milton's flagship gated address. Estate homes surround a Tom Watson-designed championship course, with indoor and outdoor tennis, resort-style pools, and a busy clubhouse calendar. Entry typically starts above $1.5M and runs well past $3M.

## Crooked Creek

A gated golf and swim-tennis community off Highway 9 with one of the widest ranges you'll find behind a gate in Milton — established executive homes through larger estate sections, all sharing the Iron Horse golf course and family-first amenities.

## Blue Valley

A newer gated enclave on the Birmingham Highway corridor: European-inspired estates on acre-plus lots with resort amenities, minutes from Crabapple and downtown Alpharetta.

## White Columns and the quiet enclaves

White Columns anchors Milton's country-club scene, and smaller gated enclaves dot the Freemanville and Hopewell corridors — addresses that often trade before they ever hit the open market.

## How gated homes really sell here

Inventory behind the gates is thin, and a meaningful share of it moves through agent relationships — coming-soon conversations, club connections, and quiet listings. If a gate, a golf course, or true acreage privacy is on your must-have list, working with an agent who watches these communities daily matters more here than almost anywhere else in North Atlanta.
