Norcross doesn't get the new-construction headlines that the far suburbs do — and that's exactly why it's worth a look. The action here isn't sprawling master-planned subdivisions; it's townhome communities and infill builds rising close to the historic downtown and along the Peachtree Corners edge.

## Why builders are here

Builders go where walkability meets attainability. Norcross has a real downtown — restaurants, green space, events — and price points that barely exist this close to a true town center anywhere else in the metro's northern arc. Most new townhomes land in the $300s to $500s.

## Who's buying

Buyers priced out of Brookhaven, Chamblee, and Peachtree Corners keep discovering that Norcross puts them ten minutes from where they were already looking, at a payment that works. New phases tend to sell through quickly because of it.

## The one rule of the sales office

The friendly on-site agent works for the builder. Bring your own representation — it costs you nothing, and it means someone is negotiating your incentives, reviewing the contract's fine print, and insisting on independent inspections before drywall and again at closing.
