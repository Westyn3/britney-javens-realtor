Most of Forsyth County is built around the car. Vickery isn't. It's one of the rare communities in the area designed around a town instead of a golf course, and that design is exactly why buyers search for it by name.

## What makes Vickery different

Vickery is built around **Vickery Village**, where restaurants, boutiques, and gathering spaces sit a short walk from many front porches. Tree-lined streets and award-winning architecture set the tone — Charleston-style facades, carriage houses, and custom homes with real architectural variety. This is the new-urbanist idea: walk to dinner, wave at neighbors, and keep the car in the garage more often.

## What the homes cost

Pricing runs from townhomes in the $500s up to custom homes well past $1M. The walkable premium is real: Vickery tends to sell above comparable square footage elsewhere in Forsyth. That's not an accident — you're paying for the lifestyle as much as the house.

## Who Vickery fits

- **Buyers coming from intown Atlanta** who want Forsyth County schools but don't want to give up walkability
- **Empty nesters and downsizers** who want a lively community with less yard work
- **Families** who like the idea of kids biking to the village
- **Anyone** who values a sense of place

## What to check before you buy

1. **Which section and builder.** Architecture and lot sizes vary across Vickery.
2. **Walkability from your specific address.** A home on the far side of the neighborhood has a very different experience than one a block from the village.
3. **HOA expectations.** Architectural standards can be stricter in design-driven communities.
4. **School zoning.** Verify the specific address with Forsyth County Schools.
