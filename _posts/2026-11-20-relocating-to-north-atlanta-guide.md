Every month I work with families relocating to North Atlanta — corporate moves, boomerangs coming home, and remote workers choosing quality of life. The challenge is always the same: how do you pick a community you've never lived near?

## Start with the three anchors

Commute (if there is one), schools (if they matter), and the Saturday test — what do you want your weekends to look like? Walkable restaurants points you toward Alpharetta's Avalon area, Roswell's Canton Street, or Brookhaven. Land and quiet points to Milton or Cumming. Value close-in points to Norcross and Duluth. Those three answers usually cut eleven communities down to three.

## Tour from your couch first

For out-of-state buyers I run video walkthroughs, neighborhood drive-alongs, and honest phone debriefs — including the things a listing will never tell you, like road noise and what the commute actually feels like at 8 a.m. Most of my relocation clients arrive for one focused in-person trip, not five scattered ones.

## The timeline trap

Relocations run on deadlines — a start date, a school semester. Build the housing search backward from that date, and decide early whether a short-term rental first makes sense. Sometimes renting for six months in the right area beats buying fast in the wrong one, and I'll tell you honestly which situation you're in.
