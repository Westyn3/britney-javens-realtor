Every January, people tell me the same thing: "This is the year." Then spring arrives, the market gets crowded, and they scramble. The buyers and sellers who do best start early and move in order. Here's a month-by-month plan for 2027 — whichever side of the transaction you're on.

## January: get honest about the goal

Write down what you want: the timeline, the price range, the non-negotiables. Buyers, decide whether you're shopping for schools, commute, land, or lifestyle — that decides the city, whether it's [Alpharetta](/alpharetta.html), [Cumming](/cumming.html), [Roswell](/roswell.html), or somewhere else. Sellers, decide whether you're listing this spring or waiting.

## February: do the money work

**Buyers:** talk to a lender and get pre-approved — properly underwritten if you can, not just pre-qualified. Know your real monthly payment, not just the sticker price. If you're a first-timer, read [First-Time Home Buyer in North Atlanta? Start Here](/blog/first-time-home-buyer-north-atlanta/).

**Sellers:** get a market evaluation and an honest list of what's worth fixing. Not every project pays back.

## March: prep and explore

**Buyers:** start touring seriously and refine your list. Winter is when you have the most leverage — see [why winter can be the smartest time to buy](/blog/winter-home-buying-north-atlanta/) — but spring brings more inventory.

**Sellers:** this is your preparation window. Declutter, handle repairs, and plan photography when the yard looks its best.

## April–May: peak season

Buyers: expect competition in popular school zones and neighborhoods. Be ready to move the day a home lists. Sellers: if you prepared, this is your payoff window.

## June–August: the family-timing window

Families often aim to move before the school year. If that's you, work backward from your target closing date — it takes longer than people expect.

## September–October: the quieter second chance

Inventory thins, but so does competition. Motivated sellers and serious buyers often meet here. My [fall market check-in](/blog/north-atlanta-fall-2026-market-update/) covers how this season behaves.

## November–December: the overlooked season

Fewer showings, more motivated parties. If you're ready, it can be a strong time to transact.

## The one thing to do this week

Pick the first domino for your situation — a lender conversation if you're buying, a market evaluation if you're selling — and book it. The plan only works if January's step actually happens in January.
