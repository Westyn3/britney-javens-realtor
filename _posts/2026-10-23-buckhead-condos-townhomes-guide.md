Buckhead means different things to different buyers. For every estate on Tuxedo Road, there are hundreds of buyers shopping something else entirely: a lock-and-leave condo near the shops, or a townhome that trades yard work for walkability.

## Why condo and townhome buyers love Buckhead

Restaurants, Lenox and Phipps, parks, and a straight shot to Midtown — Buckhead delivers a genuinely walkable life by Atlanta standards. For downsizers leaving a big North Fulton home, young professionals, and buyers who split time between cities, it's often the perfect landing spot.

## What to know before you shop

Condo and townhome buying runs on details that single-family buyers never think about: HOA financials and reserves, rental caps, assessment history, and what the fee actually covers. A building with a beautiful lobby and shaky reserves is a future special assessment waiting to happen. I review the association documents on every condo purchase my clients make — before we're emotionally committed, not after.
