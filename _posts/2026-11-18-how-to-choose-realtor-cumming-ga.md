Search "realtor in Cumming GA" and you'll get hundreds of results. Forsyth County has one of the fastest-growing agent populations in metro Atlanta — because it has one of the fastest-growing buyer populations. So how do you tell the agent who *knows* Cumming from the agent who drove up GA-400 for your appointment?

I live in Forsyth County, so I'll admit my bias up front. But these tests work no matter who you interview — including me.

## Test one: ask about neighborhoods, not the market

Any agent can recite "Cumming is growing" and "the schools are great." That's a bumper sticker, not expertise. Ask instead: *What's the difference between buying in [Vickery](/vickery.html) and buying in [Fieldstone](/fieldstone.html)?* An agent who works this county will talk for ten minutes — the walkable-village premium, HOA personalities, which swim/tennis communities like [Three Chimneys Farm](/three-chimneys-farm.html) and [James Creek](/james-creek.html) hold value because turnover is low, why [St. Marlo](/st-marlo.html) has a Duluth address but Forsyth taxes. An agent who doesn't will change the subject to interest rates.

## Test two: ask what school zoning actually does to price

Forsyth is a school-driven market — but not uniformly. The same floor plan can price tens of thousands of dollars apart across a district line, and zoning lines move as the county builds new schools. A local agent knows which communities feed which schools *today* and which rezonings are being discussed. An out-of-county agent knows what the listing sheet says.

## Test three: ask how they'd win a multiple-offer situation here

Cumming's price bands behave differently. New construction negotiates differently than resale; the $500s move differently than the $900s. You want an agent who can tell you — specifically — what terms have been winning offers in your price range in the last 60 days, not generic advice about "writing a strong offer."

## Test four: ask where their mailers land

This one sounds self-serving, and it is — but it's also real. Agents who farm specific neighborhoods (mine land in St. Marlo, Hampton Golf Village, Chattahoochee River Club, Three Chimneys Farm, James Creek, and Fieldstone every month) hear about homes *before* they list. In a county where the best resales get multiple offers in a weekend, off-market awareness is the whole game.

## Test five: check the reviews for Cumming, not just the stars

A 5.0 rating matters — but read *where* the reviews happened. An agent with reviews naming Forsyth County transactions has closed here, negotiated with the builders here, and knows which inspectors actually crawl the crawlspace.

## The honest bottom line

You don't need me to be your realtor. You need *a* realtor who can pass those five tests — because in a market moving this fast, the difference between a local expert and a licensed chauffeur is real money.
