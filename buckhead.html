Living in Buckhead

Atlanta's Premier Address,
From Estates to Skyline

Buckhead is where Atlanta keeps its landmarks: the mansions of Tuxedo Park and West Paces Ferry, the Atlanta History Center, the shops at Buckhead Village, and a dining scene that anchors the city's reputation. It's also more varied than its estate-street image suggests — the Peachtree Road corridor holds some of the Southeast's best luxury high-rise and condo living, while neighborhoods like Garden Hills, Peachtree Heights, and Peachtree Hills offer walkable streets of classic Atlanta homes.

The northern edge belongs to Chastain Park — the city's largest park, with its amphitheater concert series, golf course, horse park, tennis center, and the park-side homes that rank among intown Atlanta's most beloved addresses. Families here get an unusual combination: real intown energy with top private schools, strong public options, and green space at the door.

The market runs the full spectrum: condos from the $300s to penthouse-level, renovated bungalows and classic homes through the $1M–$3M core, and estates well beyond. Buckhead inventory is nuanced — building by building, street by street — and values turn on details that don't show up in listing photos. That's where street-level representation earns its keep.

Good to Know

Buckhead, GA
Buyer Questions

How much do homes cost in Buckhead?

The widest range in the city: condos from the $300s to penthouse-level, classic single-family homes largely $800K–$3M, and the estate streets well beyond. Values here are building-by-building and street-by-street — reach out for a current read.

What are the best neighborhoods in Buckhead?

Chastain Park for park-side living, Garden Hills and Peachtree Heights for walkable classics, the Peachtree corridor for luxury high-rises, and Tuxedo Park and West Paces Ferry for estates. It depends on whether you want a yard, a view, or a doorman.

Is Buckhead a good place to live?

It's Atlanta's premier established address — the best dining and shopping in the city, Chastain Park, strong schools, and quick access to Midtown and the Perimeter. The trade-off is price and traffic; the reward is everything else.

What is Chastain Park like?

Atlanta's largest park — summer amphitheater concerts, golf, the horse park, pools and tennis — ringed by park-side streets that are some of the most sought-after addresses inside the city. Homes there move quickly and rarely quietly.
