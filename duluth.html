Living in Duluth

The Town Green That
Every City Wants to Copy

Ask metro Atlanta planners which suburban downtown got it right, and Duluth comes up first. The Town Green and Parsons Alley transformed the city's historic center into a genuine destination — restaurants and dessert shops in restored buildings, a splash fountain and amphitheater on the green, and a festival calendar that runs essentially year-round. It gives Duluth something rare in Gwinnett: a center of gravity residents actually use weekly.

The housing bench is deep. Sugarloaf Country Club — a gated, TPC-course community of estate homes — anchors the luxury end and hosts nationally significant golf; established swim-tennis neighborhoods feed well-regarded Gwinnett schools like Peachtree Ridge and Duluth High; and townhomes plus new construction near downtown put the Town Green lifestyle within reach of first-time buyers. Duluth is also one of the metro's most internationally vibrant cities, with a Korean dining and retail scene that draws visitors from across the Southeast.

For buyers, Duluth solves a common equation: real community and real amenities at Gwinnett prices, with I-85, Peachtree Industrial, and the Gas South District's events nearby. For sellers, the demand across every price band — from downtown townhomes to Sugarloaf estates — rewards preparation and smart pricing.

Good to Know

Duluth, GA
Buyer Questions

How much do homes cost in Duluth?

Townhomes from the $300s, established swim-tennis homes largely $450s–$750s, downtown new construction $500s–$800s, and Sugarloaf estates from $1M well past $3M. A right answer at every budget — reach out for a current read.

What is Sugarloaf Country Club?

Duluth's marquee gated community — a TPC course with professional-tournament pedigree, resort amenities, and estate homes that anchor Gwinnett's luxury market. If you're shopping gated luxury east of GA-400, it's the benchmark.

What is downtown Duluth like?

The Town Green and Parsons Alley — amphitheater concerts, a splash fountain, restaurants in restored buildings, and festivals nearly every month. It's the suburban downtown other cities study, and homes within walking distance carry a premium for good reason.

What schools serve Duluth?

Gwinnett County Public Schools — including the well-regarded Peachtree Ridge and Duluth High clusters. Zoning varies by neighborhood and shifts as the county grows, so always verify for a specific address; I'm happy to check.
