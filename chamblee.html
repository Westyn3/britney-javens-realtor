Living in Chamblee

The Rail Town That
Became the Next Big Thing

Chamblee spent decades as a quiet rail-and-industry town — and then metro Atlanta noticed. The historic downtown along the rail line has transformed into one of the region's liveliest small-city centers: breweries, restaurants, and the Rail Trail connecting new mixed-use development, all anchored by Chamblee's own MARTA station on the Gold Line.

The housing story is the draw. Mid-century neighborhoods like Huntley Hills and Sexton Woods offer solid brick ranches on real lots at prices intown Atlanta can't touch, while new townhome communities keep rising around the downtown core and along Peachtree Boulevard. Add Buford Highway — the Southeast's most celebrated international dining corridor — running along the city's edge, and Chamblee delivers a genuinely distinctive quality of life for the money.

For first-time buyers, young families, and investors, the math is compelling: MARTA access, a walkable downtown that improves by the quarter, Brookhaven and Buckhead minutes away, and appreciation that has consistently outrun the metro average. The window where Chamblee is a value play won't stay open forever — that's precisely its appeal.

Good to Know

Chamblee, GA
Buyer Questions

How much do homes cost in Chamblee?

Condos and townhomes from the $300s–$500s, mid-century ranches largely $400s–$700s, and new construction from the $700s past $1M. It's the value corridor's strongest market — reach out for a current read.

Is Chamblee up and coming?

Genuinely — the downtown adds restaurants and development every year, MARTA is in place, and appreciation has consistently outrun the metro average. The 'discovery' phase is well underway, which is exactly why timing matters.

Does Chamblee have MARTA access?

Yes — its own Gold Line station downtown, which is rare and valuable: the airport, Buckhead, and downtown Atlanta without touching the connector.

Is Chamblee a good place to live?

A walkable downtown that improves quarterly, its own MARTA station, Buford Highway's dining next door, and housing value the neighboring markets can't match. For first homes and young families, it's one of the smartest buys in the metro.
