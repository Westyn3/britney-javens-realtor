Vickery
Village Living

West Forsyth's signature community — a walkable village where the lifestyle itself commands the premium.

Inside the Community

Living in
Vickery

Vickery is what happens when a community is designed around a town instead of a golf course: tree-lined streets of award-winning architecture surrounding Vickery Village, where restaurants, boutiques, and gathering spaces sit a short walk from nearly every front porch.

The homes reflect the new-urbanist vision — charleston-style facades, carriage houses, and custom builds with real architectural variety. Pricing runs from townhomes in the $500s through custom homes well past $1M, and the walkable premium is real: Vickery consistently sells above comparable square footage elsewhere in Forsyth.

For buyers coming from intown Atlanta who want Forsyth County schools without giving up walkability, Vickery is usually the first answer — and often the last stop.

Talk About Vickery

Good to Know

Vickery
Buyer Questions

How much do homes cost in Vickery?

Townhomes start in the $500s, village single-family homes run through the $700s–$800s, and custom and estate-scale homes exceed $1M. The walkable-village premium is consistent — Vickery outsells comparable square footage nearby.

Is Vickery walkable?

Yes — that's the point. Vickery Village's restaurants and shops sit inside the community, and the street grid was designed for walking first.

Where is Vickery?

West Forsyth County, with a Cumming address — convenient to Vickery Creek schools, GA-400, and the Big Creek Greenway.
